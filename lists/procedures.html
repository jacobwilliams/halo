If all the node epochs of x (halo's scaled variables) are within the range of the splined ephemeris (2 periods before the reference epoch to 2 periods after the last rev, see initialize_the_mission), less a margin of half a period. A trial point of the optimization can be far off.
equilibrate halo_eclipse_opt_submodule Subroutine

Equilibrate the continuity Jacobian at x for the optimization (eclipse_opt_equilibrate): alternately normalize its rows (weights w) and its columns (the variables' factors r, the optimization's variables being x/r), a few times, so that the rows and the columns all have 2-norms near 1. This lowers the condition number of the KKT systems of sqpopt's QP subproblems (for 100 revolutions, J's condition number goes from 8e4 to 7e2). The objective, |x/r - x0/r|^2/2, is then measured in these units.
equilibrate_scales halo_module Subroutine

Set the opt var scales from the sensitivities (xscale_mode=2): the Jacobian is computed at x (as the solver does, see jacobian_mode), and each variable's scale is divided by the norm of its column, so the columns of the scaled Jacobian all have unit norm. A unit change of any scaled variable then gives the same size change in the (scaled) constraints, so the solver's minimum-norm steps weigh each variable by its effect, not its units. (With the scales from the magnitudes, the 1e5 km position scale makes the positions at periapsis, where they are most sensitive, the cheapest variables to change.) The epoch scales are also multiplied by epoch_scale_factor.

newton_finish halo_eclipse_opt_submodule Subroutine

The Newton finish (eclipse_opt_newton_finish): from the optimization's point, solve halo's continuity constraints and, as equalities , the eclipse rows that are at or near their bound ( plus eclipse_opt_newton_active_tol), with halo's minimum-norm Newton solver (nlesolver, with halo's sparse linear solver). The optimization's point is near feasible but its continuity isn't converged; a solve of the continuity alone (eclipse_opt_polish) moves the trajectory back into the shadow, but with the active rows held at their bound it can't. A row left free that falls below its bound is added to the equalities, and the solve repeated (from where it ended).

The Newton finish's functions: halo's continuity constraints, then KS - phi_min of the active eclipse rows. A point that can't be evaluated (outside the splined ephemeris, or an integrator failure) gets large values, so the line search backs off.
