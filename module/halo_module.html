public subroutine equilibrate_scales(me, x)

Set the opt var scales from the sensitivities (xscale_mode=2): the Jacobian is computed at x (as the solver does, see jacobian_mode), and each variable's scale is divided by the norm of its column, so the columns of the scaled Jacobian all have unit norm. A unit change of any scaled variable then gives the same size change in the (scaled) constraints, so the solver's minimum-norm steps weigh each variable by its effect, not its units. (With the scales from the magnitudes, the 1e5 km position scale makes the positions at periapsis, where they are most sensitive, the cheapest variables to change.) The epoch scales are also multiplied by epoch_scale_factor.
