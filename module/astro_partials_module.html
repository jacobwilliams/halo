Analytic partial derivatives for trajectory sensitivities (state transition matrices): gravity gradients, and the time derivatives of the two-body rotating frame transformation.

These are general astrodynamics routines (they do not depend on the rest of HALO), intended to be moved into the Fortran Astrodynamics Toolkit eventually:

  • pines_gravity: spherical harmonic gravity acceleration and its gradient (the 3x3 matrix of second partials of the potential), with Pines' nonsingular formulation. It uses the same unnormalized coefficient arrays as the toolkit's geopotential_model_pines (cnm, snm), so it can be used with that class.
  • pointmass_gravity_gradient, third_body_gravity_gradient.
  • two_body_rotating_frame_matrices: the 6x6 inertial-to-rotating state transformation of the toolkit's two_body_rotating_frame (and its inverse), and their exact time derivatives.
