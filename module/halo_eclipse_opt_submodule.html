eclipse_optimize

Eclipse optimization: moves a converged solution out of the Earth's shadow.

Starting from the converged solution x0, it finds the closest continuous trajectory (in halo's scaled variables) that stays out of the shadow near the eclipses:

  • minimize
  • subject to the continuity constraints (halo's)
  • and, for each segment that comes near the shadow,

with the SQP solver sqpopt. is the cubic shadow model (Williams et al., 2023), which is smooth inside and outside the shadow. are its values at samples every eclipse_opt_sample_step seconds from the segment's start, across the part of the segment near the shadow, and KS is their Kreisselmeier-Steinhauser aggregate , : a smooth lower bound of the smallest sample (within of it), so the constraint keeps every sample at or above . One row per segment, not one per sample: the samples of a segment all depend on the same few variables (its start node), and a row for each would make the QP's working sets dependent (sqpopt's direct method then cycles).

Following the 2023 paper's process, the constraints are added only where the trajectory comes near the shadow, and the trajectory is checked again after each optimization (with new rows where needed). Each pass ends with a Newton solve of the continuity constraints and the eclipse rows at their bound, as equalities (eclipse_opt_newton_finish), to bring the defects back to the usual level without undoing the eclipse fix.

The continuity rows of the Jacobian are halo's analytic ones. The eclipse rows (7 nonzeros each: the epoch and state of the segment's start node; the samples are at fixed offsets from the start) are computed by central differences, propagating only that segment.

See docs/eclipse_optimization_plan.md.

subroutine newton_finish(d, x)

The Newton finish (eclipse_opt_newton_finish): from the optimization's point, solve halo's continuity constraints and, as equalities , the eclipse rows that are at or near their bound ( plus eclipse_opt_newton_active_tol), with halo's minimum-norm Newton solver (nlesolver, with halo's sparse linear solver). The optimization's point is near feasible but its continuity isn't converged; a solve of the continuity alone (eclipse_opt_polish) moves the trajectory back into the shadow, but with the active rows held at their bound it can't. A row left free that falls below its bound is added to the equalities, and the solve repeated (from where it ended).

subroutine equilibrate(d, x)

Equilibrate the continuity Jacobian at x for the optimization (eclipse_opt_equilibrate): alternately normalize its rows (weights w) and its columns (the variables' factors r, the optimization's variables being x/r), a few times, so that the rows and the columns all have 2-norms near 1. This lowers the condition number of the KKT systems of sqpopt's QP subproblems (for 100 revolutions, J's condition number goes from 8e4 to 7e2). The objective, |x/r - x0/r|^2/2, is then measured in these units.
